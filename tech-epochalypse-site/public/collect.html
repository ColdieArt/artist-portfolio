The Mantra

Touch the Art

Move it. The face shifts. The layers separate. What was hidden becomes visible.

"Touch the art" began with Coldie's physical, magnetic collages. Tech Epochalypse is the first time the mantra lives in a digital work. The interface turns collage from a static assemblage into a living artwork, where every viewer builds a unique composition and reshapes how the overlords are seen.

Anyone can touch the art. Only the token holder owns it.
